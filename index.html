How to read this

  • We predict is our median forecast of the lowest bid, made only from what is public before bids open: the engineer's estimate, the item list, work type, location, schedule, and recent bid results.
  • Past lettings are a backtest. Each month was predicted by a model trained only on earlier lettings, so the forecast shown is what we would have said at the time. Upcoming lettings use a model trained on everything through the latest results.
  • The 80% range should contain the winning bid about 8 times in 10. Over the full 2025–26 backtest it did so 74% of the time (73% in 2025, when prices fell faster than the model expected; 79% in the most recent six months).
  • Chance of winning is calibrated on real bids: it answers "how often did bids at this level, on projects like this, come in lowest?" "Winning" means being the low bidder; TxDOT can still reject bids.
  • Covers competitively bid TxDOT construction and maintenance lettings. Material-purchase contracts, State Use set-asides and A+B (cost-plus-time) bids are left out. Source: Texas Open Data Portal (TxDOT bid tabulations, project information, upcoming bid items, plan holders). Independent prototype, not affiliated with TxDOT.
